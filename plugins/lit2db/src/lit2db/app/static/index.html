How lit2db works

Six steps, in order. You can go back to any step you have finished.

1. Your question. Say what you want to find out, in your own words.

2. What to collect. lit2db drafts the columns of your table. You tick what you want.

3. Which papers. You see what the search finds before anything is downloaded.

4. Reading. lit2db reads each paper on this Mac, with your own Claude plan.

5. Checking. Values it was not sure about wait for you, beside the sentence they came from.

6. Spreadsheets. Three files you can open in Excel or Numbers.

Your databases are kept in your Documents folder, under lit2db. To come back later, open Terminal and type lit2db.
